Methodology & legend
  • Headline numbers: SP per_start (Stuff+ proj > rp3 data-driven > rp3 Marcel); hitter per_game (rh3, MLBAM-id-joined, collision-safe).
  • xFP RoS projects to season end (2026-09-20).
  • xFP PO = the 2026-08-17→2026-09-20 fantasy-playoff window, availability-scaled. Live IL return dates (ESPN injury_details) fold into availability; with no date, a coarse status heuristic applies — surgery / season-ending cases are over-estimated, so treat long-IL rows as ranking aids.
  • Volume-sourced rows (src ·vol) use the validated forward-volume models (2026-07-09): hitter RoS = per_PA × proj PA/team-game × team games; SP RoS = per_start × proj starts/team-game × team games.
  • The Vol column is the per-teamgame number (flat equivalents: hitter 3.5 PA/tg, SP 0.189 GS/tg). Rows without a volume row keep flat rates — SP ≈ per_start × 1.19/wk; hitter ≈ per_game × 6.3 g/wk.
  • LOW-CONF* = talent-prior fallback (Marcel if-healthy estimate for players the in-season model can't score: Judge / Greene / Snell-class IL stashes). A conviction sorter, NOT a point forecast.
  • MINE rows are highlighted.
  • ·vol in Src = RoS/PO totals use the player's projected per-teamgame volume (Vol column), not the flat league rate.
  • ·flat↓ = IL / prior-only row with no volume projection, docked to the top-quartile (p75) volume ratio of modeled rows in its universe (SP ×0.805, hitters ×0.720) so unmodeled stashes aren't credited more volume than any modeled player.
